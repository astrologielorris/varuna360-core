### Why a slider

Salaries are not the same everywhere in the world. 15 euros a month is not
the same thing in France, India, Brazil or Nigeria, and not the same for a
student as for someone earning comfortably. There is no verification, no
proof to provide, no country box to tick. I cannot check your situation and
I do not want to. Pay what you can today, and adjust later if that changes.
If 15 euros means little to you, the slider goes higher, and that directly
keeps this project alive for people who do not have the same means.
